onAI — Legal

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

onAI is a macOS menu bar utility that transforms text using AI. Privacy is a core design principle: we collect as little data as possible, and your text is never sent anywhere without your explicit opt-in.

2. Information We Collect

Purchase information. When you purchase onAI directly on our website, Lemon Squeezy processes the payment on our behalf. Your email address is collected solely to deliver your license key — we do not store it on our own servers beyond what is required for order delivery.

License validation. The app periodically validates your license key to confirm it is still active. Only the license key and a machine identifier are sent — no text content, no personal details.

Nothing else. We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, session recording tools, or any form of behavioral or device tracking.

3. How Your Text Is Processed

onAI offers three AI modes. Choose the one that matches your privacy needs:

On-Device — Apple Intelligence: Your text is processed entirely on your Mac by Apple's on-device framework. Nothing is sent anywhere — not to Apple, not to us. Requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled in System Settings.

Local — Ollama: Your text is sent to a locally running Ollama server on your Mac. It never leaves your machine. Requires Ollama to be installed and running separately.

Cloud — opt-in: If you choose to connect a cloud provider (such as OpenAI or Anthropic), selected text is sent to that provider's servers using your own API key. You will be asked to confirm this once before cloud mode activates. You can switch back to on-device or local AI at any time in Settings → Models.

Plan differences:
Basic — unlocks on-device AI (Apple Intelligence, Ollama) and lets you connect your own API key from any cloud provider.
Pro — includes everything in Basic, plus built-in OpenAI cloud access with no API key required. Your text is sent to OpenAI's servers when using this feature.
Lifetime — unlocks on-device AI and BYOK cloud, same as Basic. Does not include built-in OpenAI cloud access.

4. Your API Keys

Any cloud AI API keys you enter are stored locally on your Mac only, using secure local storage. They are never transmitted to our servers. API keys are sent only to the cloud provider you configured when that provider is actively used.

5. Accessibility Permission

onAI requires Accessibility access to read the text you select in other applications and to replace text where supported. This permission is granted in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

We only access text at the exact moment you invoke a transformation — we never monitor your screen, log keystrokes, or read text passively in the background.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the following third parties:

  • Lemon Squeezy — processes payments and manages license delivery on our behalf. Privacy Policy
  • Apple — provides Apple Intelligence and system-level APIs. Privacy Policy
  • Cloud AI providers — only involved when you explicitly opt in with your own API key. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

We may also share information to comply with legal requirements, protect our rights, or in connection with a merger or acquisition (with notice to users).

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • All network connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • API keys are stored using secure local storage on your Mac
  • We have no backend servers that store your text or usage data

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We recommend not entering highly sensitive information (passwords, financial data) into any AI tool.

8. Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. License status is managed by Lemon Squeezy. All app preferences and API keys live on your Mac only. You may request deletion of any data we hold by contacting us at koszhanov.tilek@icloud.com.

9. International Data Transfers

When using Cloud AI mode, your selected text is sent to the provider you configured (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic), whose servers are typically located in the United States. This transfer occurs using your own API key and is subject to that provider's privacy policy. On-device (Apple Intelligence) and local (Ollama) modes involve no international data transfers.

To exercise these rights, contact us at koszhanov.tilek@icloud.com.
