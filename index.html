Privacy Policy

onAI is built to keep your data on your device.

Overview

onAI is a macOS menu bar utility that transforms text using AI. Privacy is a core design principle: we collect as little data as possible, and your text is never sent anywhere without your explicit opt-in.

What We Collect

Purchase information. When you subscribe or make a one-time purchase, Apple and RevenueCat process the transaction. We receive only an anonymous identifier — never your name, email address, or payment details.

Apple Search Ads attribution. If you discovered onAI through an Apple Search Ad, anonymous attribution data may be shared with RevenueCat for analytics. This data cannot identify you personally.

Nothing else. We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporters, session recording tools, or any form of behavioral or device tracking.

How Your Text Is Processed

onAI offers three AI modes. Choose the one that matches your privacy needs:

ON-DEVICE
Apple Intelligence
Your text is processed entirely on your Mac by Apple's on-device framework. Nothing is sent anywhere — not to Apple, not to us. Requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled in System Settings.
LOCAL
Ollama (local server)
Your text is sent to a locally running Ollama server on your Mac. It never leaves your machine. Requires Ollama to be installed and running separately.
CLOUD
Cloud AI (opt-in)
If you choose to connect a cloud provider (such as OpenAI or Anthropic), selected text is sent to that provider's servers using your own API key. You will be asked to confirm this once before cloud mode activates. You can switch back to on-device or local AI at any time in Settings → Models.
⚠️ Important: A subscription to onAI unlocks the app and all on-device AI features — including Apple Intelligence and local models via Ollama. Cloud AI requires your own API key from the respective provider (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic) and is not included.
Your API Keys

Any cloud AI API keys you enter are stored locally on your Mac only, using the system keychain or secure local storage. They are never transmitted to our servers. API keys are sent only to the cloud provider you configured when that provider is actively used.

Accessibility Permission

onAI requires Accessibility access to read the text you select in other applications and to replace text where supported. This permission is granted in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

We only access text at the exact moment you invoke a transformation. We never monitor your screen, log keystrokes, or read text passively in the background.

Third-Party Services
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription entitlements on our behalf. RevenueCat Privacy Policy →
  • Apple — handles App Store purchases, Apple Intelligence, and receipt validation. Apple Privacy Policy →
  • Cloud AI providers — only involved when you explicitly opt in with your own API key. Each provider has its own privacy policy.
Data Retention

We do not store any user data on our servers. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat and Apple. All app preferences and API keys live on your Mac only.

Children's Privacy

onAI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available here and in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy.

Subscription & Billing

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time from System Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the next renewal date.

What the Subscription Includes

Your subscription or lifetime purchase unlocks the onAI app and all on-device AI features — including Apple Intelligence and local models via Ollama. It does not include:

  • Access to any third-party cloud AI service (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)

Cloud AI features require a separate API key from the chosen provider, paid directly to that provider under their own terms.

How the AI Works
ON-DEVICE
Apple Intelligence
Runs entirely on your Mac. Private, free, and offline. Requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled in System Settings. Model assets may take a few minutes to download after first enabling it. Response speed depends on your Mac's workload and thermal state.
LOCAL
Ollama
Runs AI locally via a server on your Mac. Requires Ollama to be installed and running. Performance varies by the model you choose and your hardware.
CLOUD
Cloud AI (your API key)
Sends selected text to the provider you configure using your own API key. Typically delivers the fastest, highest-quality results. Requires a valid, funded API key from the chosen provider.
