2. What We Collect and Why

Email address — collected by Lemon Squeezy at checkout solely to deliver your license key. We do not store your email on our own servers. Legal basis: performance of a contract (delivering the product you purchased).

License key — the app periodically sends your license key and a Lemon Squeezy instance ID to verify the license is active. No text content or personal details are included. Legal basis: legitimate interest (preventing fraud).

Mac name — sent once to Lemon Squeezy only when you first activate your license (e.g. "John's MacBook Pro"). This lets you see your active devices in the license portal. It is stored by Lemon Squeezy and is not sent again during subsequent validations. Legal basis: legitimate interest (one-device enforcement).

Nothing else. No usage data, no text you transform, no identifiers beyond the above.

3. How Your Text Is Processed

onAI offers three AI modes:

On-Device — Apple Intelligence: Your text is processed entirely on your Mac by Apple's on-device framework. Nothing is sent to Apple, to us, or anywhere else. Requires Apple Intelligence to be enabled in System Settings.

Local — Ollama: Your text is processed by a locally running Ollama server on your Mac. It never leaves your machine. Requires Ollama to be installed separately.

Cloud — your own API key (all plans): Selected text is sent to the provider you configure (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) using your own API key. We never see or store this text. Each provider's own privacy policy applies.

Cloud — built-in OpenAI (Pro plan only): Selected text is sent to OpenAI's servers via our account. We do not log or store this text. OpenAI's Privacy Policy applies to text processed through this feature.

4. Your API Keys

Any cloud AI API keys you enter are stored locally on your Mac. They are never transmitted to our servers. Keys are sent only to the cloud provider you configured, only when that provider is actively processing a request.

5. Accessibility Permission

onAI requires Accessibility access to read the text you select in other applications and to replace it where supported. This permission is granted in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

We access text only at the exact moment you trigger a transformation. We never monitor your screen passively, log keystrokes, or read text in the background.

6. Third Parties We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal information. The only third parties that receive any data are:

  • Lemon Squeezy — processes payments and delivers license keys on our behalf. Receives your email and payment details at checkout. Privacy Policy
  • Apple — provides Apple Intelligence and macOS system APIs. Privacy Policy
  • Cloud AI providers — receive selected text only when you actively use cloud mode with your own API key. Each provider has its own privacy policy.
  • OpenAI — receives selected text when Pro subscribers use the built-in OpenAI feature. Privacy Policy

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

7. International Data Transfers

The following international transfers may occur:

  • Lemon Squeezy — your email and payment data may be processed on servers in the United States.
  • Cloud AI providers — when using cloud mode, your selected text is sent to the provider's servers, typically in the United States.
  • License activation — your license key and Mac name are sent to Lemon Squeezy (typically US servers) once at activation. Subsequent validations send only the license key and an anonymous instance ID.

On-device (Apple Intelligence) and local (Ollama) modes involve no international data transfers whatsoever.

8. Data Security
